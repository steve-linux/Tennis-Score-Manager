New Remotes mode: no wristbands needed, two cheap Bluetooth selfie remotes paired with the phone like a keyboard are enough. One click = point, double click, long press or the other button = undo. During the match the app must stay open with the screen on. New Padel option: same rules as tennis, with doubles and golden point already set, or FIP star point. Summary with statistics (aces, faults, winners and errors too) and point by point. Wristbands with firmware 2.4: stroke recognition.
