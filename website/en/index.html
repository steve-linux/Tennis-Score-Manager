Your tennis score, like a real Pro.

The Android app that keeps the score by the ITF rules and calls it out loud like a chair umpire. Score from the phone or from two Bluetooth wristbands, and show the result live on a TV.

Features

Everything you need on court

Made for real matches, even without an umpire: tap who won the point and the app does the rest.

ITF rules

Singles and doubles, tie-breaks, 10-point match tie-break, No-Ad deciding point, changes of ends and serving order, always right.

Umpire voice

The phone calls the score, games, sets and changes of ends in your language, even offline. You can also use your own recordings.

TV scoreboard

A second phone, a tablet or a computer shows the score live on a TV or monitor, with match and break clocks.

Bluetooth wristbands

With two M5StickS3 on the wrist, each player scores their own point with a button, and the phone can stay in the bag.

History and summaries

At the end of the match you get the result, duration, points and games won and the place: save them as text, data or image in the folder you want and share them with any app. Matches can be suspended and resumed, even after closing the app.

Six languages, everywhere

Italian, English, French, German, Spanish and Portuguese: the chosen language applies to the screens, the voice, the scoreboard and the wristbands.

TV scoreboard

A show on court

Any screen becomes a scoreboard, with no special hardware and no internet.

The app includes a small web server. The two devices just need to be on the same Wi-Fi network: one phone's hotspot is enough, and no mobile data is used.

Wristbands

Score from your wrist

Two M5StickS3 connect to the phone over Bluetooth LE. Each player presses KEY1 on their own wristband to win the point (KEY2 undoes it): the app counts it, calls it and updates the scoreboard.

  • Score on the wristband displayIn the same language as the app, with adjustable brightness.
  • Name and settings from the phoneGive each wristband a name and adjust brightness, volume and automatic power-off; one tap copies them to the other.
  • Battery under controlThe app shows the charge and estimates how long it lasts; at the end of the match it switches the wristbands off.
  • Ready in a momentSwitch the wristbands on and the app finds them by itself; «Identify» tells you which is which.
  • Free firmwareThe Arduino code is in the repository: install it with the Arduino IDE.

ITF rules

The score follows the ITF rules: no more remembering who serves or when to change ends.

  • Two formats: best of three sets with a tie-break to 7 at 6-6, or two sets and, at one set all, a 10-point match tie-break.
  • Singles and doubles, with the team's serving order chosen at every set.
  • No-Ad: if you like, a single deciding point is played at deuce.
  • Coin toss and sides of the court are set before you start; the app tells you at every change of ends.
  • Safe undo: the match is stored as a sequence of points and recomputed at every undo, so server, side and score are always exact, even when you undo several points in a row.
  • Match messages: break point, set point, match point and deciding point appear on the phone and on the scoreboard, along with tie-breaks and changes of ends.

Umpire voice and languages

During the match the phone calls points, games, sets and changes of ends, with a chair umpire's wording and the correct pronunciation in each of the six languages.

Three ways to give the umpire a voice

  • The phone's text-to-speech: choose one of the installed voices; those marked «offline» work without internet.
  • Pre-generated audio files: the app prepares every call in advance, for a more natural reading that is fully offline.
  • Your own recordings: record the phrases with your voice (or an umpire's) and import them as a ZIP file; they always take precedence.
Six languages: Italian, English, French, German, Spanish and Portuguese. The chosen language applies at once to the screens, the voice, the TV scoreboard and the wristband display.

TV scoreboard

An LED-style scoreboard, dark and high-contrast, with names, points, games, sets, match time and serve countdown. Team colours and the bottom line (for example club and court) can be customised in the app. Choose what to show (match time, timers, completed sets, messages, ball next to the server) and check the result in the preview on the phone. If the connection drops, the scoreboard reconnects by itself.

How to connect

  • Put the two devices on the same Wi-Fi network: one phone's hotspot works perfectly, and no mobile data is used.
  • On another phone with the app, tap «Use as scoreboard»: it connects by itself.
  • On any other device, scan the QR code or open the address shown by the app in the browser.
  • For the TV, use an HDMI cable or screen casting (Chromecast, Smart View…).
ITF clocks: 25 seconds between points, 90 at the changeover, 120 at the end of a set.

Bluetooth wristbands (M5StickS3)

Leave the phone at the side of the court: with two M5StickS3 wristbands each player, or team, scores the point by pressing KEY1 on their own wristband, and KEY2 undoes it.

  • Everything in sync: press the button and the app counts the point, calls it and updates the TV scoreboard. Every press is acknowledged, so no point gets lost.
  • Display in your language: the wristband shows the score and messages in the app's language.
  • Battery: the app shows the charge of each wristband and estimates how long it lasts, and the wristband warns you when the battery is low. At the end of the match and when you exit, the app switches them off; the side button turns them back on.
  • Settings from the phone: name (up to 12 characters, stored in the wristband), brightness, how long the score stays on the display, buzzer volume, flipped display for the other wrist and automatic power-off. One tap copies them to the other wristband.
  • Automatic search: switch the wristbands on and the app finds and pairs them by itself. «Identify» flashes the one you pick, «Swap P1 ↔ P2» swaps the players without pairing again, and the match can also start with KEY1.
  • Phone in your pocket: even with the screen off, the app keeps receiving the points, calling them out and updating the scoreboard.
The firmware is free software (GPL-3.0) and is installed with the Arduino IDE: code and instructions are in the repository.
